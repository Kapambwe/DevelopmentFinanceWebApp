# Development Finance Sample Data Index

Pattern:
`wwwroot/sample-data/{Country}/{InstitutionType}/{InstitutionName}/{fileName}`

## Countries Included

- Botswana
- Ghana
- Kenya
- Namibia
- Nigeria
- South_Africa
- Tanzania
- Zambia

## Coverage Notes

- Development finance bundles include institution info, verifiable credentials, dynamic forms, digital tokens, and in some cases accounts and ledger data.
- Zambia includes value-chain facility, partner bank, implementation partner, citizen empowerment, accelerator, and venture capital samples.
- World now includes an IFAD agricultural value-chain programme bundle with stakeholder data.
- World now includes a Sustainable Livestock Infrastructure Management Project bundle covering livestock productivity, infrastructure commercialisation, and institutional support.
- World now includes a Climate Resilient Livestock Management Project bundle covering climate adaptation, productivity gains, gender inclusion, and livestock value-chain resilience.
- Sample data is loaded by JSON-backed mock services and follows the existing folder naming convention with underscores.
